About
24 BLVD

Created by Davis Corporation, 24 BLVD is Bangkok's most vibrant dining and entertainment destination, where food, music, culture and community come together. Located within The Davis Bangkok Hotel compound on Sukhumvit 24, it runs from leisurely brunches and afternoon drinks to live performances, international DJs and late nights.

24 BLVD brings locals, expats and travellers together, drawing thousands of people to Sukhumvit 24 every night and making the neighbourhood one of Bangkok's most exciting social destinations.

Three rooms, three sounds

Home to three distinctive nightclubs spanning house, tech house, techno, global sounds, R&B, hip-hop, Jersey club and open format, Sukhumvit 24 offers an ever-evolving calendar of international DJs, live performances and exclusive events. Drawing thousands of people each night, it is an immersive playground for adults, where every corner offers a new discovery and every night becomes an adventure.
